Operations research on Montréal's open transportation data, with each project framed as an airline problem transposed to a city.

Experiments, maps and optimization models built on public data from the Ville de Montréal, the STM, the ARTM, BIXI and Statistics Canada. Where a model needs something that isn't public, the assumption is labelled as illustrative on the page.
