Culture: a timeline and a map

Every unit of the course ends with a culture interlude. Here are all sixty: on a timeline from the Göktürks to today, and on a map of Turkey's seven regions. Each card opens its interlude and leads back to its unit.

How this page is made

Each card sums up its interlude in a sentence or two, using only what the interlude itself says: open the interlude for the whole story and its sources. Dates are given as the interlude gives them, and “As tradition tells it” marks a legend or a tradition's own account.

The timeline proper starts with the Göktürks, in the 6th century. The interludes about older times are folded at the top, and customs with no date of their own sit at the end, under Today.

The map's borders come from Natural Earth, which is in the public domain (Made with Natural Earth). The regions are the seven drawn by the First Geography Congress in 1941, with each province in the region it is usually listed under. The 1941 boundaries cross some provinces, so a place near a border can sit in another region on other maps: Hattusa, in Çorum province, is listed under the Black Sea region here, with its province. Pins mark places named in the interludes.
